<a id="overview"></a>
That's Not My Neighbor free download brings you an immersive gaming experience that combines social deduction with simulation elements. In this full version for Windows, you play as a building's doorman responsible for stopping doppelgangers from entering your neighbors' apartments. Every decision you make has consequences, making it vital to pay attention to the smallest details.

The game features engaging mechanics that require you to inspect documents and behaviors of visitors at the reception. This captivating premise keeps you on your toes, especially as you observe the unique behaviors of characters, including their blinking patterns.
